# Things to talk to the co-teachers about

Open questions that need a history teacher's judgment, not a developer's. Add freely; strike through when settled
and move the answer to `DECISIONS.md`.

## Maps and projections

1. **Projection per focus.** Afro-Eurasia currently uses Equal Earth (flat, equal-area, centred on 60°E). The
   alternative is a Lambert azimuthal view that looks like a globe seen from above Central Asia. Pictures:
   `progress/2026-10-29-1450.png` and `progress/2026-10-29-1450-lambert.png`. Proposal: Equal Earth for wide
   east–west bands (Afro-Eurasia, world), azimuthal for compact focuses (Africa, Indian Ocean, East Asia),
   Albers for mid-latitude bands (North America). Does that match how you teach them?
2. **Region definitions.** The "focus on region" list should use the AP World framework's World Regions
   (East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Central Asia, Middle East / Southwest Asia, North Africa, Sub-Saharan
   Africa, Europe, the Americas, Oceania) with the framework's own boundaries. Confirm the list and where the
   edges fall (e.g., is Afghanistan Central Asia or Middle East; is Egypt North Africa or Middle East).
3. **The 1450 base map.** The source atlas has no 1450 map. 1400 still shows the Mongol Great Khanate over
   China and no Moscow; 1492 shows Ming and Moscow but already has Constantinople as Ottoman. Parchment uses 1492
   and says "base map 1492" in the corner. Agree?
4. **Border certainty before 1648.** Every pre-1648 border is drawn dashed (approximate) by default. Which Unit 3
   polities should instead be shown as a soft sphere of influence with no line at all (Songhai? the Safavid east?).

## Content and alignment

5. **Which polities are highlighted per unit.** v0 highlights the eight Unit 3 states the CED names. For other
   units, who decides the list, and where does the list live (a tab in the Google Sheet is the plan)?
6. **Subdivisions.** The layers idea needs internal boundaries: Ming/Qing provinces, Ottoman eyalets, Mughal
   subahs, Tokugawa domains, Songhai provinces. Which polities need this for the way you teach centralization,
   in what order? Each one is a separate data source to find and license.
7. **Reasoning-skill readings.** Comparison, continuity/change, and cause/effect items are written by teachers,
   not generated. Who drafts them per unit, and what does a finished one look like (length, required evidence,
   citation style)?
8. **A better word than "things".** On-map items are *marks* (cities, events, fills). The margin items that carry
   a reasoning skill need a name. Candidates: *readings*, *moves*, *arguments*, *notes*. Pick one.

## Classroom use

9. **Review-day routine.** The reading-routine panel has four prompts (two observations, one context, one cause,
   one thing the map cannot support). Right prompts? Right order?
10. **Chromebook reality.** Fits 1366×768 without horizontal scroll. Does anyone's class use a smaller screen?
